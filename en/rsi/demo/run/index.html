What are Gen 0 / Gen 1 / Gen 2
Gen = Generation = "which generation of AI wrote the code".
· Gen 0 starting point: The original, unmodified BM25 code (word-by-word matching) hits roughly 30-45%.
· Gen 1: The first version of improved code the AI wrote after reading Gen 0's list of failed queries.
· Gen 2: The AI read Gen 1's failed queries and revised the code again.
Every generation is new Python code generated live by AI — not filled in from a template.
31 questions in 3 difficulty tiers (+ 5 hold-out questions)
E · Easy · 8 questions: solvable with BM25 weighting alone (apple 128gb latest → iPhone 15 128GB, usbc charger 65w).
M · Medium · 8 questions: Ones with exclusion terms or mixed Chinese and English (queen sheets no polyester → 100% cotton options, iphone 15 but not pro).
H · Hardest · 15 questions: Functional intent, without a single product term in the sentence (A quiet gift for long-haul flyers → noise-canceling headphones, warm clothes for winter hiking → down jacket, Listening to music with friends at the beach → Bluetooth speaker).
Also 5 hold-out problems: The AI never sees these 5 queries at any point; they're used to quietly check whether it truly generalizes.
⚠️ Toy-scale concept demo · not a production benchmark · for real deployment use N ≥ 200 per tier
What is NDCG (scoring method)
Not "did it get it right" (binary right/wrong), but "how good is the ranking" (a continuous 0~1 value). For each question, the AI returns its top 5 candidates:
· Correct answer ranked Rank 1 → 1.00
· Ranked #2 → 0.63　·　Ranked #3 → 0.50　·　Ranked #5 → 0.39
· Top 5 are all wrong → 0.00
We use a continuous score so every small improvement shows up. Otherwise, even as Sonnet slowly improves, the score just jumps between 0 and 1 and looks stuck.
The 6 numbers in the verdict row
NDCG — Average ranking score across the 31 questions.
E / M / H — Average NDCG across the 3 difficulty tiers (lower scores on harder tiers indicate a weaker model).
heldout — NDCG on 5 questions the AI has never seen.Overfit models break down here.
perturbed — The 31 questions rerun with typos added (apple → aplpe). Anything that cheats with a lookup table gets exposed.
combined — Final fitness score; > 0 means promotion. The formula includes E/M/H weighting + hold-out 20% + perturbed 10%.
