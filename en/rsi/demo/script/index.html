What these 30 seconds cover · 4 choices

Three anti-cheating gates
  • 31 public questions: visible to the AI · main score
  • 5 hold-out problems:Never seen by the AI · catches rote memorization
  • Typo · 31 questions: Randomly swap characters in each word · catches lookup-table cheat sheets (`apple → aplpe`)
  • Blended score = 0.7 × public + 0.2 × hold-out + 0.1 × typo − code penalty
